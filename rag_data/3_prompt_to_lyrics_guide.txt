Prompt-to-lyrics guide (use these conventions):
- Start with a vivid setting line (city/room/time-of-night)
- Follow with 2–3 short verse lines that match the genre vibe
- Add a memorable hook phrase near the end
- Keep lines short, rhythm-friendly, and emotionally consistent
Examples of hooks:
- "stay close in the neon glow"
- "we’ll study through the silent hours"
- "heartbeats in the bassline"
